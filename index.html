          Install on your iPhone

          Checking installation status…

          1. Open your deployed HTTPS address in Safari.
          2. Tap the Share button.
          3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
          4. Enable Open as Web App (if shown), then tap Add.

          This installs a web app icon and standalone experience, not an App Store IPA. The microphone runs only when the app is open.

          Learned Sounds

          For one sound, use Reset on its card in Learn. Full reset erases this gun's learned sounds and reset/noise/beep calibration. Its saved results and other guns' data stay intact.

          Backup & Data

          Export all gun profiles, learned numeric sound features, drill presets, recognition history, saved sessions and preferences. The backup contains no raw recordings. Keep this file private.

          Import replaces saved guns, sounds, history, presets, sessions and preferences on this device. Older backups contain no saved sessions. Files from another device may perform differently.

          Audio processing · this gun

          Changes apply to the next capture. Audio processing may affect recognition and require fresh examples; it never deletes your learned sounds.

          Appearance & feedback · this device

          When enabled and supported, vibration is requested at GO and repetition completion.

          Delete local app data

          Deletes every saved gun, learned sound, calibration, preset, setting and history on this browser. Export a backup first. This action cannot be undone without a backup.

          About

          Dry Fire With Ease

          Sound trainer · v0.8.0

          Dry-fire practice,
          made simple.

          Use your device's microphone to recognize your learned trigger click, time your repetitions and review your progress.

          A simple practice routine

          1. Learn your trigger click.
          2. Choose a practice mode or drill.
          3. Follow the start signal and review your saved results.

          Keep separate profiles for up to five guns, create custom drills and view timing trends. Other sound categories are optional unless your drill uses them.

          Saved on your device

          No account is required. Your profiles, learned sound features, drills and history stay in this browser. Use Settings → Export backup to keep a recovery copy before changing device or clearing browser data.

          Practice responsibly

          Complete the safety check before recording or practice. Sound recognition is experimental. It cannot verify an unloaded firearm, aim or safe handling, and is not a calibrated measurement instrument.
