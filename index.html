        Checking installation status…

        1. Open your deployed HTTPS address in Safari.
        2. Tap the Share button.
        3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
        4. Enable Open as Web App (if shown), then tap Add.

        This installs a web app icon and standalone experience, not an App Store IPA. The microphone runs only when the app is open.

        Learned Sounds

        For one sound, use Reset on its card in Learn. The button below erases all learned sounds for the selected gun. Its history and other guns' data stay intact.

        Backup & Data

        Export all gun profiles, learned numeric sound features, saved drill presets and recognition history. The backup contains no raw recordings. Keep this file private.

        Import replaces saved guns and their training data on this device. Files from another device may perform differently.

        About

        Dry Fire With Ease v0.8.0 · Sound trainer · Private IndexedDB storage · Up to 50 learned examples per sound and configurable last-N recognition results. This is experimental and is not a calibrated measurement instrument.

        Microphone access stops when you tap Stop, leave recognition, or the page becomes hidden. Gun profiles, numeric learned features, custom drills and recognition results are stored in this browser's IndexedDB. No raw audio is saved or uploaded. Clearing browser storage erases local data.
