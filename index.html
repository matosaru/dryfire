How it works
  1. Record at least 3 examples each of Draw from holster, Rack slide, Trigger pull and Change magazine.
  2. Choose a built-in drill or free listening, then start the microphone and check detections.
  3. Verify predictions to measure accuracy over time.

01 / TRAIN THE MODEL

Learn your sounds

Capture at least 3 samples of each type (8–10 recommended). Each capture gives you a two-second countdown followed by a two-second recording window.

Recording tip: Keep the phone in a consistent position, perform just one action per recording, and avoid background noise. Raw audio is not saved—only numeric sound features.

      Backup & restore

      Export learned numeric sound features and the saved result history. The backup contains no raw recordings. Keep this file private.

      Import replaces the model and history on this device. Files from another device may perform differently.

      About

      Sound Trainer v0.7.0 · Private IndexedDB storage · Up to 50 learned examples per sound and configurable last-N recognition results. This is experimental and is not a calibrated measurement instrument.

      Microphone access stops when you tap Stop, leave recognition, or the page becomes hidden. Only numeric learned features, predictions and optional verification labels are stored in this browser's IndexedDB. No raw audio is saved or uploaded. Clearing browser storage erases local data.
