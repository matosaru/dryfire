# Competitor analysis and research evidence

CHG-026 / CHG-073 · Updated 2026-11-12. This is a dated first-party website snapshot, not a complete product test or current EU price survey. Do not quote unverified prices in product copy or treat competitor payment acceptance as provider/legal clearance for EasyDryFire.

## Research scope and method

Compare the complete cost and steps to a first useful repetition, not just an app download price. Record country/currency/taxes, check date, hardware requirements, free capability, paywall/subscription terms, sign-in/calibration steps, backup and statistics, cancellation/restore, and optional device requirements. Separate software-only subscriptions from hardware purchase paths. Preserve first-party links and distinguish public claims from hands-on observations.

## Implications for EasyDryFire

- **Microphone-trigger timing alone is not unique.** Dry Fire Pro explicitly advertises ML trigger detection and a shot timer. Do not claim EasyDryFire is the only microphone-based trainer.
- **Validate a narrower difference.** Candidate distinctions are user-trained trigger sound profiles, local-first/browser availability, privacy-minimized audio processing, and no required proprietary laser hardware. The sampled competitor homepages do not prove competitors lack any of these or establish EasyDryFire accuracy superiority. Customer interviews and hands-on comparison are required before positioning claims.
- **Keep phone-only and hardware competitors separate.** Compare time/setup to first useful repetition and total annual cost. Laser/sensor equipment prices should be shown in a separate category from app subscriptions.
- **A relevant localized price anchor is now visible, but not a recommendation by itself.** The Dry Fire Pro App Store listing displayed €2.99 monthly, €22.99 yearly and €44.99 “Pro Tier” in Lithuania; its US listing showed $2.99, $19.99 and $39.99. The product label does not establish whether Pro Tier is lifetime, and the native-store offer is not directly equivalent to EasyDryFire web billing. App Store currency/price displays are localized storefront evidence and can change. The submitted analysis's €4.99/month and €49–€69 lifetime and the existing €24/year EasyDryFire candidate remain hypotheses. The annual candidate is near the observed €22.99 native-store price but is not validated for web purchase, demand, customer-country tax, provider eligibility, storage/support cost or contribution margin. None should be published before these are checked.
- **Do not assume all advanced controls are paid elsewhere.** Dry Fire Pro markets starter drills, drill-building tools and HQ statistics in its free feature summary, while Premium advertises its full drill library, Aim coaching and unlimited custom drills. This is a useful packaging comparison: basic custom practice or simple progress may need to remain Free for EasyDryFire to be compelling; the final split should reflect observed customer value and recovery costs.
- **Do not expand scope to keep up feature-for-feature.** Prioritize trigger readiness, first-session guidance and actionable recognition diagnostics. Camera coaching, clubs and extra sound categories are not initial launch requirements.
- **Preserve simple packaging.** The project constraint is one Pro plan. Multiple monthly/yearly/lifetime choices or feature packs would add decisions and support complexity; compare one billing cadence at a time before approval.

## Remaining research before price selection

1. Inspect Dry Fire Pro's in-app paywall and renewal/cancellation/restore flows on a supported Lithuanian device; confirm the €44.99 Pro Tier term and which drills/statistics are actually free versus Premium.
2. Repeat store-page capture for countries selected for EasyDryFire launch and record date, currency, taxes shown, trial and offer term; the Lithuanian listing is one comparator only.
3. Run hands-on comparisons for phone-only versus laser/sensor setups: time to first repetition, calibration burden, offline operation, export/recovery, accessibility and support. Do not infer reliability/recognition quality from marketing copy.
4. Request written provider eligibility, fees, payout, refunds/disputes, tax/invoice roles and accepted product/marketing descriptions for EasyDryFire. Competitor provider use does not establish our eligibility.
5. Replace all hypothetical net-cost inputs with the actual contract, customer-country tax treatment, measured storage/auth/support use, refunds and fixed business expenses.
6. Conduct a small, nonbinding pricing-comprehension and willingness-to-pay study with target users. Separate stated interest from actual conversion evidence; do not use competitor prices as a substitute.

No competitor artwork, copy, trademarks or drill content should be copied. Keep source, date and captured evidence with any future pricing claim. See the [monetization rollout plan](monetization-rollout.md) for the decision sequence.
