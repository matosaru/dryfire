## Working conclusions to validate

- Differentiate through a short foreground-phone flow: add a gun → train Trigger → start. Do not require hardware, a social account or a subscription to try basic microphone practice.
- Prioritize first-trigger reliability and clear countdown/STOP feedback over a large paid feature catalog. A microphone timer cannot promise the movement/aim data of other sensors.
- Offer one Pro upgrade for convenience and deeper insight, with plain language explaining its exact shipped benefits. Avoid separate sound packs, gun tiers, training bundles and multiple billing cadences at launch.
- Keep export/import and access to personal saved results available. Pro recovery should solve a clear failure case, such as browser data being cleared, rather than using confusion about storage to sell an upgrade.
- Test actual onboarding with new users and the physical iPhone target. Record time to first practice, permission/calibration errors and whether users can explain Free versus Pro in one sentence.

These are product recommendations based on this app's scope, not proven competitor weaknesses. No competitor artwork, copy, trademarks or drill content is to be copied.
